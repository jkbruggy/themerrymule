Fir-quently Asked Questions!

Everything you wanted to know

Ordering

Do I have to buy the tree first?

That's up to you! Buy your tree at any lot and we'll swing by to pick it up. Or add our $15 shopping service, and we'll shop the lot with you over a video call, load your pick into the mule cart, and bring it right to your door.

Which tree lot can I choose?

Any lot in Mule Territory! All we need is a tree lot and an address, and we'll get The Merry Mule moving.

How do I book a delivery?

Fill out our request form, use our Quick Form, or call or text (843) 564-3104. We'll reach out to confirm the details.

How does the Tree Shopping Service work?

We head to the lot and video call you from the trees. You pick your favorite, pay for the tree, and we bring it home. It's $15 per 15-minute call, paid before the call starts.

How many trees and wreaths can I order?

Up to 2 trees and 4 wreaths per customer. That's what fits in The Merry Mule in one trip!

Can I order just a wreath?

Not by itself. Wreath delivery is an add-on to a tree delivery, so we'll bring your wreaths home along with your tree.

Pricing and payment

How much does delivery cost?

$50 for your first tree within 15 miles, and $40 for a second tree. Wreaths are $5 each when added to a tree delivery. Tree and wreath prices are set by the lot and are separate from our fees.

How do I pay?

After we confirm your request, we send a secure Square invoice. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay all work. Here's when each part is due:

  • Tree and wreath delivery: paid within 24 hours of your delivery.
  • Tree Shopping Service: your $15 must come through in Square before your video call starts.
  • A tree or wreath we buy for you: paid in full before we buy it at the lot.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes! Military, Veterans and first responders get $5 off their first tree delivery and $2.50 off their first wreath. Just let us know when you book. The discount doesn't apply to the Tree Shopping Service.

Does it cost anything to call or text?

Nope! Calls and texts are always free. The only call we charge for is a Tree Shopping video call, which is $15 per 15 minutes.

Do you keep my card information?

Never. We don't ask for or keep card numbers anywhere. Every payment runs through Square. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Can I cancel my delivery?

Yes, anytime, for free. Plans change, and that's okay!

How do I reschedule?

Text MOVE IT to (843) 564-3104 and we'll find a new time that works for you.

What if I need to cancel my Tree Shopping call?

Every Tree Shopping call comes with one free reschedule. Cancel at least 24 hours before your call and we'll refund your $15 in full.

What if I cancel after you've bought my tree?

We'll return it to the lot and refund the purchase price, minus $10 for our time and trip. That's true even if we're already on the way. We'll just turn around.

What if the weather is bad?

If we need to move your delivery for weather or safety, rescheduling is always free and you pick the new time. Full details are in our Terms of Service.

Tree care

How do I keep my tree fresh?

Great question! Here are our top 5 tips:

  • If more than 6 to 8 hours have passed since the lot cut your tree, saw about half an inch off the bottom before it goes in the stand.
  • Use a stand that holds about 1 quart of water for every inch across the trunk, check it daily, and never let the water drop below the base.
  • Plain tap water is best, no add-ins needed.
  • Keep your tree away from fireplaces, heaters, vents and sunny windows.
  • Use low-heat LED lights and turn them off at bedtime.
