Fir-quently Asked Questions!

Good questions (FAQs):

Do I have to buy the tree first?

That's up to you! Buy your tree at any lot and we'll swing by to pick it up. Or add our $15 shopping service, and we'll shop the lot with you over a video call, load your pick into the mule cart, and bring it right to your door.

Which tree lot can I choose?

Any lot in Mule Territory! All we need is a tree lot and an address, and we'll get The Merry Mule moving.

How do I pay?

After we confirm your request, we send a secure Square invoice. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay all work.

Is The Merry Mule locally owned?

You bet! The Merry Mule was founded in 2026 by a Veteran right here in Charleston, and we're based out of Johns Island. Keep an eye out, you might spot the mule hauling trees through your neighborhood!

Does it cost anything to call or text?

Nope! Calls and texts are always free. The only call we charge for is a Tree Shopping video call, which is $15 per 15 minutes.

Do you set the tree up inside?

Not right now. To keep our prices tree-sonable, we drop your tree off at your front door, ready and waiting when you get home. If enough folks ask, we may add inside setup in the future!
