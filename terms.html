1. What we do

The Merry Mule is a Christmas tree delivery service in the Charleston, South Carolina area. We offer:

  • Front Door Delivery. You buy your tree at a local lot, and we pick it up and bring it to your front door. $50 for the first tree within 15 miles, $40 for a second tree.
  • Tree Shopping Service. We video call you from the lot so you can pick your tree from home. $15 per 15-minute call, added to your delivery.
  • Wreath Delivery. $5 per wreath, added to a tree delivery. Wreaths are add-on only, so we don't deliver wreaths by themselves.

We deliver up to 2 trees and 4 wreaths per customer. Tree and wreath prices are set by the lot and are separate from our fees. Our current delivery area is listed on our home page. If you're not sure you're in Mule Territory, text us and we'll let you know.

Military, Veteran and first responder discount: $5 off your first tree delivery and $2.50 off your first wreath. Let us know when you book. The discount doesn't apply to the Tree Shopping Service.

2. Payment

We bill through Square, our secure payment processor. We never ask for or keep your card details. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay all work.

  • Tree and wreath delivery: due within 24 hours of your delivery.
  • Tree Shopping Service: the $15 fee must come through in Square before your video call starts. We can't begin the call until it does.
  • A tree or wreath we buy for you: the lot's full price is paid before we buy it. We don't front the cost of trees or wreaths.

3. Cancellations, rescheduling and refunds

Our Promise Plans change. Cancel any delivery for free before we hit the road.

Delivery only (you bought your own tree)

You can cancel anytime, free of charge. Since delivery is paid after it happens, there's nothing to refund. To pick a new day, text MOVE IT to (843) 564-3104 and we'll work out a new time with you.

Tree Shopping video calls

  • Every Tree Shopping call comes with one free reschedule.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled call and we'll refund your $15 in full.
  • Cancel less than 24 hours before your call and the $15 isn't refundable, but you can still use your free reschedule if you haven't already.
  • If we're running behind because of a scheduling conflict, we'll text you to let you know. A late start doesn't make the call refundable, and you still get your full 15 minutes.

A tree or wreath we buy for you

  • Once we buy a tree or wreath for you, it's yours, and the lot's price can't be refunded as-is.
  • If you cancel your delivery after we've bought it, we'll return it to the lot and refund the purchase price, minus $10 for our time and trip.
  • This applies even if we're already on the road. Just call or text us, and The Merry Mule will turn around.

Weather and our own delays

If The Merry Mule has to move your delivery because of weather, safety or anything on our end, rescheduling is always free and you choose the new time.

How to cancel or reschedule

Call or text (843) 564-3104, text MOVE IT to reschedule, or email haulidays@themerrymule.com. Refunds go back through Square to your original payment method.

4. Delivery day

  • We drop your tree at your front door, netted and ready for the stand. We don't knock or ring the bell, so you don't need to be home.
  • We don't carry trees inside or set them up right now.
  • Once your tree is on your porch, it's in your care. We aren't responsible for weather, pets, wildlife or anything else that happens to it after drop-off.
  • Please tell us about gates, gate codes, steps or anything else we should know in the special instructions when you book.

5. The trees themselves

We don't grow or sell trees. The lot is responsible for the tree's price, quality, size and freshness. When we shop for you, we'll show you the tree on video before we buy it, so the final pick is always yours. We handle every tree with care, but natural trees can drop needles and settle in transit, and that isn't damage.
