Photo and Video Release

Last updated November 14, 2026

Thanks for being part of The Merry Mule! We love sharing happy deliveries with our community. Please read this release, and if you agree, fill in the form at the bottom. Your typed name counts as your signature.

What you're agreeing to:

  1. Permission to capture. You allow The Merry Mule to take photos and videos of you, anyone with you, your tree and wreaths, and the outside of your home during our time together, including pickups, video shopping calls and deliveries.
  2. Permission to share. You allow The Merry Mule to use those photos and videos, along with your first name, to promote the business on our social media accounts (including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and our Google Business Profile), on our website and in our marketing.
  3. Tagging. If you choose below, you allow us to tag you and/or your business in those posts.
  4. Editing. We may crop, edit, caption or add music to photos and videos. We'll never use them in a way that's meant to embarrass you or misrepresent you.
  5. Your privacy. We won't post your full address, house number or phone number.
  6. No payment. You won't be paid for the use of these photos or videos, and The Merry Mule owns the photos and videos it takes.
  7. Changing your mind. You can ask us to stop using your photos or videos at any time by emailing haulidays@themerrymule.com or texting (843) 564-3104. We'll remove posts we control within a reasonable time. We can't pull back content that others have already shared or downloaded.
  8. Kids. If anyone under 18 appears, you confirm you're their parent or legal guardian and agree on their behalf.
  9. Age. You confirm you're at least 18 years old.
